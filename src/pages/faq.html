About Nelview.

What the platform does and how it differs from other tools.

What exactly is Nelview?
An AI peer-review and research-evaluation platform. Upload a manuscript, grant, proposal, thesis or abstract and get a structured, publication-level review with a 0–100 readiness score across methodology, novelty, statistics and ethics, plus prioritised recommendations.
How is it different from Grammarly or ChatGPT?
Those tools focus on grammar and generating content. Nelview doesn't write your paper. It evaluates it the way a reviewer, supervisor or editor would, against the standards that decide whether work gets published.
How is Nelview different from Google's Paper Assistant Tool (PAT)?
PAT is a Google Research tool built to catch mathematical and logical errors in computer science papers. So far it has been offered free to authors through pilots at specific conferences such as ICML and STOC. It deliberately doesn't score or rank papers. Nelview is built for every discipline and every stage of research: theses, grant proposals, ethics applications and journal manuscripts, not only CS conference papers. You get a 0–100 readiness verdict with prioritised next steps, journal-fit and predatory-journal checks, the option to add a named human expert, and pricing and supervisor workflows designed for African universities, in English and French. The two tools share a view we agree with: AI should help authors catch problems before submission, not replace peer review.
What document types can it review?
Journal manuscripts, grant proposals, research proposals, theses and dissertations, ethics applications, conference abstracts and book proposals. The type is detected automatically.
Can I get a real human review too?
Yes. On any verdict you can commission named experts matched by field and h-index: one reviewer, two, or a full editorial panel. Double-blind, with a signed report in 5–14 days.
Does it support African journals and contexts?
It's core to our mission. The formatter and Journal Check cover African and international venues, and legitimacy is verified against DOAJ and COPE.
Is my work safe and private?
Your document is processed only to produce your review. It is never used to train models and never sold. You keep full ownership of your work.

Plans and payment.

Pricing is being finalised for launch.

How much does Nelview cost?
Pricing is being finalised for launch. Tell us about your needs and we'll share the plan that fits, including founding-member rates for early users. Contact us.
I'm a student at a partner university. Do I pay?
No. Your institution licenses a seat for you. Sign up with your university email and you'll join its workspace.
What happens to my account after I graduate?
With your supervisor's approval you can move your projects to a personal account on any plan and keep your full review history.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Card, mobile money, bank transfer and PayPal. Institutions can pay by invoice.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Your plan runs to the end of the billing period, and you keep all your projects.

For universities.

Seats, criteria and rollout for departments and faculties.

How is institutional pricing calculated?
Per licensed seat per academic year, with volume tiers. Supervisors and admins are free. Pilots for a single programme are available for one semester.
Do students ever pay?
No. Students on a licensed seat get unlimited AI reviews under your criteria and never see a paywall.
Which systems do you integrate with?
SAML and Google SSO, plus Moodle and Canvas. Student rosters can be imported by CSV or synced from your LMS.
Where is our data stored?
Documents are processed only to produce reviews, never used for training, and never sold. Data residency options are available for institutions that need them.
What happens when students graduate?
With supervisor approval, graduates can move their projects to a personal Nelview account and keep their review history. Their seat returns to your pool.

For reviewers.

Reviewing for Nelview as an expert.

Who can apply?
Professors, postdocs and research consultants with a verifiable publication record and peer-review experience in their field.
How and when am I paid?
Payment is held in escrow when you're awarded a brief and released to your payout account once the author accepts your report.
What does verification involve?
ORCID and affiliation checks, a publication review and a short identity (KYC) step required for payouts.
Can I review for institutions too?
Yes. Partner universities can invite approved experts as external reviewers for theses and high-stakes submissions.
